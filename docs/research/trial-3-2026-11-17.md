## Improvements (ranked; not built yet)
1. **Skip the source's intro/trailer when ranking** (`clips.auto`, `highlights.candidates`): podcasts open with a
   1–2 min montage of their best lines plus title cards. Detect it (dense scene cuts and on-screen text in the first
   ~3 min, or the same lines repeated later) and rank the original occurrence instead.
2. **Prefer the full original moment over the trailer copy:** when a candidate's words also appear later, pick the
   later, uncut occurrence (more context, no burned titles).
3. **Burned-in source text and subtitles:** `understand` already finds a burned caption band; use it at clip creation
   (move our captions off it, or crop/mask it as a listed decision) and warn when a crop cuts through on-screen text.
4. **Caption fragments:** section transcripts can start mid-word ("-UP"); start the caption track at the first whole
   word and strip leading hyphen fragments.
5. **Hook title suggestions:** every auto clip lacks a hook title (costs 5–10 retention points). Keep it
   agent-written, but have `clips.auto` list 2–3 suggested hook lines (opening question / strongest line) in
   `fixes_suggested` with the exact `overlay add-text` command.
6. **Clips 2 and 3 score 57–58:** both open on the host's question with a long answer. Suggest `edit cold-open` with
   the strongest answer line in `fixes_suggested`.
7. **`long_silence` on clip 3 after `remove_pauses`:** align QC's silence rule with the cleanup threshold, or make the
   fix target what QC flags.
8. **camera_motion on clip 1:** caused by the montage's many cuts; goes away with improvement 1.
