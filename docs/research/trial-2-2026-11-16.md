## What is worse or broken (exact repro)

1. **`clips auto` export names collide and overwrite silently.** Names are `auto-<rank>-<mm>m<ss>s` of the range start,
   with no source or clip id. Repro: `clips auto --source-id src_01m7exnsgke5zpnzdnmj22jxq7 --input-json @{"ranges":[{"start":3.07,"end":41.07}]}`,
   then the same with `src_01m7exq0r5cm1k7zgdb6rqys6v` 3.07–56.89 and `src_01m7exv5s1vnz97ftc2ekvmdah` 3.24–102.55.
   All three export to `exports/auto-1-00m03s.*`; the last run's mp4/report/srt/post/cover replace the others; no
   warning (`warnings: []`). Five runs left three export names. Expected: a unique name (e.g. clip id or source title
   suffix) or a refusal/warning. The clip names collide too (three clips named `auto-1-00m03s`).
2. **The `trim_tail` fix cut the last word.** Repro: `clips auto --source-id src_01m7extd0g7e180kfnpj4nh83v` with range
   2.14–26.96 → applied `edit trim … --segment-id seg_01m7j6w7yd7akct43tcg10t3tj --end-delta -1.11`; afterwards
   `clip retention` reports `ending.kind: hard_stop`, last line "…the gifts and talents that you actually" ("have." is
   gone; ending 6 → 0 points). The source transcript is captions-based (smart defaults say "word timings are
   approximate"). Expected: never trim past the last word's end; skip `trim_tail` when timings are approximate;
   re-check `ending.kind` after the fix and undo it when it got worse.
3. **Template effects from `clip create --template` (and `clips auto`) are not tagged `by_template`, so applying the
   template again stacks a second set.** Repro (clip 05 after removing out-of-range effects):
   `template apply podcast-viral --clip-ids clp_01m7j6wxbjbfr1fr1jjx8mkvcy --dry-run` → patch *adds* `fx_24` 5.0–6.5,
   `fx_25` … next to the existing `fx_1` 5.0–6.5 (`effect list` shows `by_template: null` on fx_1–fx_16). The
   templates guide says re-applying replaces them. Consequence: a hook added after creation cannot be restyled with the
   template without doubling the punch-ins.
4. **Shortening a clip leaves effects past the end, and then `template apply` fails.** Repro: on clip 05 run
   `edit delete-segment` five times (81.69 → 48.78 s); `effect list` still has fx_10–fx_16 and fx_24–fx_28 at 50–81.5 s;
   `template apply podcast-viral --clip-ids clp_01m7j6wxbjbfr1fr1jjx8mkvcy --dry-run` exits 2:
   "/tracks/effects/9: 50.00 .. 51.50s is outside the clip (0 .. 48.78s); …". Expected: edits drop or clamp effects that
   fall outside, or the validator ignores them consistently (`edit cold-open` did remove one such effect itself).
5. **Smart layout split ≈1 s after the real shot cut.** Clip 04: smart defaults split at source 20.80 s; the cut to the
   close-up is at ≈19.8 s (trial 1 split at 19.80). `render preview-frame clp_01m7j6w4dc2c8f1dggznbtj2jn --out-t 17.9`
   (source 20.04) shows the close-up letterboxed in blur-fit. Fixed by hand with `edit split … 17.66` +
   `clip set-layout --preset center-crop` + `crop track-face`.
6. **`export clip --render-id <older render>` mixes files from two states.** Repro: after directing clip 04,
   `export clip clp_01m7j6w4dc2c8f1dggznbtj2jn --render-id rnd_01m7j6w87200c0fx6460t52d4w --name v2-04-ten-vs-three-auto`
   → the mp4 is the old render (byte-identical to `auto-1-00m02s.mp4`) but the `.srt` starts "You're as good" (current
   cold-open timeline; the video starts "but think about") and the cover/post are the current ones. I copied
   `auto-1-00m02s.srt` and `.cover.jpg` over the two mismatched files so the export set is consistent.
7. **`remove_pauses` reports `ok: true` but changes nothing** when timings are approximate (clip 04: no new revision,
   segments unchanged, measured silence still 2.9 s → 3.3 s after directing; silence 0/10 on 04 and 05).
8. **`caption keywords --auto` misclassifies**: sentence-initial words become names ("You're", "Three.", "Definitely" on
   04; "Vietnamese", "Western" on 01); the number word "three" is not a number. Its default colour `#2BFF5E` equals the
   hormozi-pop active-word green, so marked keywords look the same as the karaoke highlight.
9. **`clip cover` makes the render stale for retention.** Right after `clips auto` (which ends with the cover), `clip
   retention` on 04 reports `measured_on: timeline`; on directed 01 the score moved 94 (render) → 93 (timeline) after
   cover/meta/score with no edit.
10. Smaller: the smart-defaults decision for 04 lists the same override command twice; the `clips auto` report has no
    `fixes_suggested` field at all (the guide says text pops and cutaways are listed there); 02 keeps a 0.275 s blur-fit
    fragment (12.405–12.68) between two layouts; the copied project's QC paths still point to
    `C:\Users\krishna\rustler-real1\…` (absolute paths in stored QC records).

Visual weaknesses that are tuning, not bugs:

- **Graphics in blur-fit are unreadable** (02 "5-minute mirror", 04 the 10/10 vs 3/10 bars). Trial 1's hand crop of
  the central 54 % x 78 % was clearly better; v2 needed `layout edit-region` by hand.
- **Punch-in density**: template "every 5 s" (1.12, 1.5 s) plus retention punch-ins in between = 19 on a 51 s clip
  (02), changes every ~1.2–2.5 s, all with the same amount; a template punch-in starts 0.1 s before a layout cut (02,
  t≈5.0); punch-ins on graphic segments crop the graphic's text (04 directed, t = 10 and 15).
- **Face at the frame edge** on some frames: 02 t = 15/20 (auto and directed), 05 auto t = 30.6 (half a face),
  05 directed t = 42.7.
- **Hook sizes differ by style**: hook-big 108 px (0.10), hook-box-yellow and hook-question 92 px (0.085) on one line,
  which reads small next to hook-big.
- **Source pop-ups** (subscribe cards) are in 3 of 5 auto clips; `mask add --kind blur` hides the text but leaves a
  bright smear; `clip cover --auto` picked a frame inside the masked cold open on 03 (re-picked with `--out-t 9.3`).
- `clips auto` adds no hook title (by design), which caps the hook at 10–20 of 25 points.

## Ranked recommendations for quality tuning

1. Make `clips auto` export names unique (source short title or clip id), and warn before overwriting any export.
2. Guard the tail/head trims: never cut inside the last/first word; skip trims when word timings are approximate;
   re-measure `ending.kind` and roll back a fix that makes the ending or hook worse.
3. Tag template-created effects `by_template` at `clip create --template`, and drop/clamp effects outside the clip after
   any edit that shortens it (or have `timeline.apply` prune them), so a template can be re-applied safely.
4. Graphic shots: when the planner sees graphics (flat/edges), crop to the graphic's bounding box (like trial 1's
   region) instead of whole-frame blur-fit, and do not put punch-ins on graphic segments.
5. Punch-in pattern: replace fixed "every 5 s" + extra retention punch-ins with one plan that snaps to stressed words,
   keeps ≥0.5 s from cuts, varies the amount (1.08–1.2) and targets ~4–5 changes per 10 s, not 8–9.
6. Framing: clamp look-room so the face box never leaves a margin of ~8 % of the width, and centre punch-ins on the face.
7. Shot boundary precision: refine smart-layout splits to the frame-accurate cut (04 was ~1 s late).
8. `clip cover --auto`: make it faster (14–18 s is ~half of an auto run), avoid masked/overlay regions, and do not mark
   the render stale.
9. Keywords: skip sentence-initial capitals as names, add number words, and use a colour distinct from the caption
   preset's active-word colour.
10. Pop-ups: offer a detector or at least a `mask` preset for the bottom-card area; prefer a box/crop over blur for
    white cards.
11. Hook styles: a common minimum size (0.10) across hook presets, or apply the template's hook size to hooks added later.
